What to react to

  1. Overall vibe. Does this read as "Linear-clean, non-AI"? If anything feels too generic, too AI, or too cold, say so.
  2. Accent colour. Deep teal (#1F5862). Used sparingly. If you want a different teal, a different colour entirely, or a no-accent monochrome, swap is mechanical.
  3. Density. Spacing, type sizes, card padding. Tighter? Looser?
  4. Functional layout. Detail panel on the right (vs modal). Mobile tap-menu. Empty-state CTAs. Archive as a list (vs columns). Users panel with invite form inline at the top.
  5. Anything missing. Affordances or screens we didn't think to mock up.
